## Overview

MyPerfectVanilla (MPV) is a meticulously curated modpack designed for players who love the core spirit of Vanilla Minecraft but crave modern performance, breathtaking visuals, and seamless quality-of-life improvements. 

Instead of overwhelming the game with complex tech trees or custom magic systems, MPV focuses on polishing what makes Minecraft great: exploration, building, and immersion.

---

## Key Features

* **Next-Gen Performance:** Powered by Sodium, Lithium, and modern optimization engines to maximize your FPS and eliminate stuttering, even with high render distances.
* **Stunning Visuals:** Fully compatible with iris shaders and features custom, subtle resource tweaks that enhance textures, leaves, and water without breaking the vanilla art style.
* **Quality of Life:** Includes seamless inventory management, advanced tooltips, an intuitive map/minimap system, and a zoom key.
* **Immersive Audio:** Enhanced ambient sounds and directional audio bring caves, forests, and oceans to life.
* **Pure Vanilla Compatibility:** Built to work perfectly on standard vanilla multiplayer servers. Play with your friends anywhere!

## Installation Guide

Getting started is quick and easy:

1. **Download a Launcher:** We recommend using the Modrinth App or CurseForge.
2. **Search for MPV:** Type "MyPerfectVanilla" into the search bar.
3. **Install & Play:** Click "Install", wait for the assets to download, and hit "Play"!

> Tip: For the absolute best experience, make sure to enable your preferred Shader Pack in the Video Settings menu after launching!
